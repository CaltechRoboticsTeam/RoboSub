Left stick: forward/back and strafe. Right stick: turn and up/down. RT rolls right; LT rolls left. Click Enable to use the inputs, and Disable to stop.

Hold A: forward 1 m, then hold position/depth. Hold B: hold depth. Hold X: 0.5 m deeper. Hold Y: turn 90° clockwise and hold heading/depth. Release to end a maneuver. Stick or trigger input takes over immediately. Assistance needs fresh sensor feedback; A also needs DVL position.

LB/RB decrease/increase effort by 1% for hover or 5% for drag; wait 2 seconds between taps. Positive effort is up/forward. Modes start at zero; tap the same mode button to exit.
